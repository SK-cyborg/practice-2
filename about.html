Our Mission

EcoTech 2035 was founded on a simple premise: technology caused many of our environmental crises, but guided by the right hands, technology is also the only way out.

We are a coalition of engineers, environmental scientists, and volunteers dedicated to accelerating the transition to a sustainable economy. By bridging the gap between cutting-edge technology and environmental stewardship, we empower communities and businesses to implement solutions that restore our ecosystems.
